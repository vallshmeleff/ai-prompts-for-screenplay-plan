This platform hosts a variety of materials-including projects, plans, narratives, hypotheses, and dialogues-intended for the development of sci-fi screenplays for films, television series, or video games. These materials are rooted in cutting-edge hypotheses, speculative theories, engineering concepts, and innovative ideas.
While not all the content is scientifically verified, that remains our goal.
The materials are presented in a format suitable for training AI; certain episodes are described from multiple perspectives, which allows for more precise training of language models.
The primary characters are Jett, Steeve, and Central Command (an AI that governs everything). The story is set in the distant future, though the narrative occasionally references our own present day.
Central Command-a super-intelligent AI-manages all aspects of life on the planet, orchestrating various projects and research initiatives. Through its investigations, Central Command uncovers evidence suggesting the existence of a distributed, cosmic-scale "Virtual Consciousness" and begins gathering information about it. Suspecting the existence of "Transition Points" between the virtual realm and the physical world, Central Command sets out to locate them.
To this end, Central Command enlists the help of Jett, a versatile specialist who finds the subject compelling. Together with his colleague Steeve, Jett begins exploring this phenomenon, uncovering a wealth of fascinating scientific and technical data.
